Nobody Does It Better

“A Symphony of Taste in Every Bite”

VN Wrap n Roll makes premium quality Vietnamese food, made in Australia — from slow-simmered noodle soups to hand-rolled rice paper rolls, marinated proteins, hearty rice and noodle meals, and fresh salads.

We supply Asian supermarkets and wholesale partners with prepacked, ready-to-eat products, and run our own takeaway shops where the same recipes are cooked fresh to order. Every product is snap-frozen at peak flavour with no added preservatives, so what you get is as close as possible to a bowl made from scratch.

Our range keeps growing — every new product goes through the same process: sourced fresh, cooked the traditional way, and packed to travel from our kitchen to your freezer without losing what makes it worth eating.

How we work
